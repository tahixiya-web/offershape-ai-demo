Important modelling assumptions. Energy prices are user-entered and illustrative by default. Off-peak covers weekends and 21:00–07:00 weekdays. The indexed shock is applied to an assumed NZ$160/MWh reference for the indexed share; it is not a price forecast. Daily and demand charges, and illustrative excess-volume charges are modelled; pass-through charges, network nuances, tax, losses, curtailment, fixed-volume hedging, project capex and legal terms require separate review. Short datasets are annualised. This is decision support, not a binding quote, risk advice or engineering assessment.
