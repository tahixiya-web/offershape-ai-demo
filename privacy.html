Privacy of this public demo

This standalone demonstration runs its energy comparisons and CSV import locally inside your browser. It does not collect email addresses, process payments, create accounts, or send project data to OpenAI or a custom server.

If you use “Save project”, the data is stored on that browser/device using localStorage and can be removed with the app’s Delete control or your browser’s site-data controls. Your hosting provider may log standard web requests and IP addresses. Web fonts are loaded from Google.

Use illustrative or expressly authorised data only. The modelling engine is an early-stage screening tool, not independent commercial or legal advice.
